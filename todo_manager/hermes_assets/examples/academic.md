# Example conventions: academic work

## What becomes a task

- Reviews I agreed to do (an accepted invitation, an assignment by the editor or chair) are tasks
  with the review deadline as due date.
- Thesis chapters, reports and drafts my students send me for feedback are tasks; the due date
  is the date we agreed, else one week after receipt.

## What to dismiss

- Review invitations I have not accepted: dismiss (label `review-invitation`); once I accept,
  the review becomes a task.
- Calls for papers and submission invitations not addressed to me personally: dismiss
  (label `cfp`).

## Questions, reminders and follow-ups

- Ask me before creating a task for grant, hiring or committee requests whose owner is unclear.
- Follow up with co-authors after one week without a reply.
